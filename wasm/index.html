HOW TO PLAY
Click the game window, then press ENTER to launch.
Arrow keys / WASD: thrust. SPACE: fire. P: pause. R: restart.
Q / ESC: menu. T: theme. M: mute. F: FPS. H: help. I: local leaderboard.
1-3: difficulty / upgrade. X: reset statistics.
Letters / Backspace: leaderboard initials.
